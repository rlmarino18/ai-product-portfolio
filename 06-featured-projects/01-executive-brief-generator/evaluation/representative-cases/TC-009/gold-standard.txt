## 1. Executive Summary

Crestview is managing two recent employee safety incidents, including a ladder fall that resulted in an emergency-room visit and a chemical splash incident. Monica ordered three additional ladders of the same model removed from service immediately pending Victor's inspection. The causes of both incidents remain unresolved, and managers were instructed not to communicate unconfirmed causes. External reporting requirements for the ladder incident are under review. A client-facing factual incident summary is due Wednesday and requires preparation, review, and Monica's approval before release.
